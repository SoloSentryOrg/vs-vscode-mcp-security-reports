# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, evidence-led, and mindful that security and
privacy discussions can affect real people and organisations.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, publishing personal or confidential information, and disclosing
uncoordinated vulnerability details.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other project spaces.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's platform reporting controls for abuse. For sensitive
project-related concerns, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting
details publicly.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that do not follow this
standard and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project
or its community.
